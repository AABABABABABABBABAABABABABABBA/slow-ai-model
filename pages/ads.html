Ad provider setup

Free users on your site see ads. Each provider is activated by setting its env var on the chadgpt Pages project. Paid Plus users never see ads.

Quickest path

The three that approve instantly and work out of the box:

  1. Sign up at a-ads.com — crypto payout, zero review. Copy your ad-unit id.
  2. Sign up at publishers.adsterra.com — PayPal payout, usually instant approval.
  3. Sign up at partners.propellerads.com — PayPal payout, popunder + display.

All supported networks

How to set a key

After signup, grab the required value from the provider's dashboard and run:

export CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN=cfut_...
npx wrangler pages secret put <ENV_KEY> --project-name chadgpt
# paste the value when prompted

Non-sensitive keys (adsense client id, a-ads unit id) can go in pages/wrangler.toml [vars] instead of secrets — whichever is handier.

The ad slot refreshes as soon as you reload the page. First provider wins (future: rotate per impression).
